🎯 Concentration Risk

Nasdaq 100: Top 10 ≈ 50% (Mag 7 heavy).
Asia ETFs: China/Taiwan concentration.

Mitigation: Blend with broad World or S&P 500 ETFs.

🏭 Sector / Region Skew

Nasdaq: ~60% Tech.
Asia: Heavy China/HK exposure.

Mitigation: Diversify across regions and sectors.

📉 Volatility

Nasdaq 100: ~20% vol, –22% max DD.
Asia: ~18% vol, –25% max DD.

Mitigation: Size positions to your risk tolerance.

🔄 Synthetic Risk

XSPU uses total return swaps.

Mitigation: Prefer physical replication (SPYL, CSPX, VUAA).

💧 Liquidity Risk

Small AUM ETFs (APEX, A50D) have wider spreads.

Mitigation: Use limit orders. CSPX/SPYL are liquidity kings.

💱 Currency Risk

USD, HKD, EUR exposure for non-US investors.

Mitigation: Use hedged share classes if desired.

🚫 Tax Drag (CRITICAL)

NEVER buy US-domiciled ETFs (QQQ, SPY, VOO). 30% WHT + US estate tax >$60K.

Ireland UCITS = 15% WHT + no estate tax.

📋 Accumulating Tax Complexity

Some jurisdictions tax "deemed distributions" on Acc ETFs.

Mitigation: Check local tax code — Dist may be simpler.
