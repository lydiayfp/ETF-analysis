🎯 Concentration Risk

Nasdaq 100: Top 10 holdings ≈ 50% of index (Mag 7 heavy).
S&P 500: Top 10 ≈ 35%.

Mitigation: Diversify across both indices or add small-cap/value tilt.

🏭 Sector Skew

Nasdaq 100: ~60% Technology.
S&P 500: Broadly diversified across 11 sectors.

Mitigation: S&P 500 is the natural hedge if you already hold Nasdaq 100.

📉 Volatility & Drawdowns

Nasdaq 100: ~20% annualized vol — expect –20% to –30% drawdowns.
S&P 500: ~15% annualized — milder drawdowns.

Mitigation: Match position size to your risk tolerance and time horizon.

🔄 Synthetic / Swap Risk

XSPU uses total return swaps — counterparty risk with DWS.

Mitigation: Cap synthetic exposure or prefer physical replication (SPYL / CSPX / VUAA).

💧 Liquidity Risk

XNAS has lower volume than CNDX.
XSPU has only ~12K avg daily volume.

Mitigation: Use limit orders. CSPX/SPYL are the liquidity kings.

💱 Currency Risk

All are USD-denominated — non-US investors face USD/EUR or USD/GBP FX swings.

Mitigation: Use EUR-hedged share classes (e.g., CSP1) if you want to eliminate FX exposure.

🚫 Tax Drag (CRITICAL)

NEVER buy US-domiciled ETFs (QQQ, SPY, VOO) as a non-US person. You lose 15% extra in withholding tax (30% vs 15%) and face US estate tax on holdings >$60K.

Ireland UCITS = 15% US WHT + no estate tax exposure.

📋 Accumulating Tax Complexity

Dividends are reinvested internally, but some jurisdictions tax "deemed distributions" annually anyway.

Mitigation: Check your local tax code — distributing share classes may be simpler in some countries.
